I’m Varinder Singh, a full-stack developer and B.C.A. student at Gian Jyoti Institute of Management and Technology in Punjab.

I started web development training in 2024. Instead of stopping at exercises, I kept building complete products — a bakery store, a dental clinic booking system, a salon platform, a gym membership site, a pet store and an AI assistant. Each one has a real backend and an admin panel, and each one was deployed live. I deliberately used different stacks — React, Vite, Python with FastAPI, PHP with Tailwind and Alpine.js — so I’d understand each properly.

In 2026 that practice turned into my first client. A local bakery was taking every order by phone and WhatsApp, so I built them Premium Cakes: a full ordering platform with a no-code admin panel, launched on their own domain. I still support it today.

Now I’m looking for an internship or entry-level role on a team where I can keep shipping real things — and I take on freelance projects for local businesses.
