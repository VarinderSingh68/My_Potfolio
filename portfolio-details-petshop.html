Overview

Happy Tails is a full-stack store for a pet supply business. Customers shop by the animal they care for, save a profile for each pet, and book real appointment slots for grooming, boarding and vet visits — all from one site.

The part I’m proudest of is on the owner’s side. Instead of a dashboard that only reports what already happened, the admin panel includes a Growth Copilot that reads order history and surfaces concrete next steps, such as which customers are due for a reorder or haven’t tried a subscription yet.

What I built

Shop by pet

Browse by dog, cat, bird or fish, so customers only see what fits their animal.

Pet profiles

Saved profiles let the store estimate when a bag of food is likely to run out.

Real booking slots

Grooming, boarding and vet visits booked straight from the services page.

Owner dashboard

Revenue, order status and new customers at a glance.

Growth Copilot

Analyses order history to flag reorder-due customers and subscription opportunities once there’s enough data.

Server-rendered & light

PHP pages styled with Tailwind, with Alpine.js for interactivity instead of a heavy framework.

What this project shows

  • Server-side rendering with PHP 8 on Apache
  • Utility-first styling with Tailwind CSS and lightweight interactivity with Alpine.js
  • Turning raw order data into actionable suggestions for a business owner
