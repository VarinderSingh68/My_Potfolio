About This Project

Bakery Business Growth Agent is a full-stack e-commerce platform built for a bakery business, covering both the customer-facing storefront and the operations side that runs it.

On the storefront, customers can browse a categorized product catalog (cakes, cupcakes, pastries, donuts, cookies, muffins and more), pick size or weight variants, add items to a cart or wishlist, and get help from a live chat widget. On the admin side, I built a dashboard that gives full control over orders, products, categories, promotional banners and offers, plus a built-in analytics view tracking page views, product views, cart additions, wishlist activity, and an exit-intent popup with A/B test tracking.
