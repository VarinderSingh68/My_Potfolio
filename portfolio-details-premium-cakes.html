About This Project

A complete, production-deployed e-commerce site built for a local bakery. It replaced manual phone and WhatsApp ordering with a self-service online storefront, plus a no-code admin dashboard the owner runs day to day without any developer involvement.

What It Does

Customers browse a live menu of 200+ products across 25+ categories — cakes by flavor, bento cakes, savouries, breads, add-ons and more — each with size/weight-based pricing, then add items to a cart and check out. Every order generates a PDF invoice and notifies the owner automatically via WhatsApp and email. A discount coupon system, plus “60-minute delivery” and “eggless” badges, highlight promotions and dietary info right on the menu.

Admin Dashboard

The owner runs the entire business from a password-protected admin panel — no code, no developer needed for day-to-day changes:

  • Add, edit and price products and categories
  • Upload and swap homepage banner images
  • Create and manage discount coupons
  • View and fulfill incoming orders
  • Update store settings (contact info, delivery terms, etc.)

Technical Highlights

  • Custom frontend in vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no framework overhead, fast load times
  • Node.js/Express backend with a PostgreSQL database
  • Self-hosted on a Linux VPS (Nginx reverse proxy, PM2 process management, free auto-renewing SSL)
  • Client-side data layer with offline-friendly local caching that syncs to the cloud, so the storefront stays fast even on a slow connection
  • Automated order confirmation emails and PDF invoice generation
  • Fully responsive, mobile-first layout with a custom brand color system
  • Migrated the bakery’s full historical product catalog and order history from a previous hosting provider with zero data loss

My Role

I designed and built the entire product end-to-end — frontend, backend, database, admin tooling and deployment — then handled the full production launch (domain, hosting, SSL). Since the sale I’ve continued to provide ongoing iteration and support, shipping new features, design changes and bug fixes as the client’s needs evolve.
