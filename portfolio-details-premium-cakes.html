The problem

Well Baked Bakery took every order by phone and WhatsApp. That meant typing out menus and prices by hand, confirming each order in chat, and relying on a developer for any change to what the website showed.

They needed a real online storefront customers could order from themselves — and a way for the owner to run the menu, prices, promotions and orders without touching code.

What I built

A live, searchable menu

200+ products across 25+ categories — cakes by flavour, bento cakes, savouries, breads, add-ons — each with size- or weight-based pricing.

Cart, checkout & coupons

Customers order on their own, with a discount coupon system for promotions.

Automatic invoices & alerts

Every order generates a PDF invoice and notifies the owner on WhatsApp and by email.

Badges that sell

“60-minute delivery” and “eggless” badges put promotions and dietary info right on the menu.

An admin panel the owner actually uses

The owner runs the business from a password-protected dashboard — no code, and no developer needed for day-to-day changes:

  • Add, edit and price products and categories
  • Upload and swap homepage banner images
  • Create and manage discount coupons
  • View and fulfil incoming orders
  • Update store settings such as contact info and delivery terms

Under the hood

  • Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end — no framework overhead, fast on the phones customers actually use.
  • Node.js and Express backend with a PostgreSQL database.
  • A client-side data layer with offline-friendly caching that syncs to the cloud, so the store stays quick on slow connections.
  • Self-hosted on a Linux VPS behind an Nginx reverse proxy, managed with PM2, with free auto-renewing SSL.
  • Fully responsive, mobile-first layout with a custom brand colour system.

Launch & support

I handled the full production launch — domain, hosting and SSL — and migrated the bakery’s full product catalog and order history from its previous host with zero data loss.

Since the sale I’ve kept supporting the platform, shipping new features, design changes and fixes as the business’s needs evolve.
