Overview

Ordering medication online requires significantly higher trust, compliance, and operational precision than standard e-commerce. Patients need to find treatments by specific health conditions, upload prescriptions securely for licensed pharmacist review, and verify cold-chain delivery.

I engineered HomeMed as a full-stack digital pharmacy platform built with PHP and MySQL, deployed live on Alwaysdata. The system connects patient-facing features — disease categorization, prescription uploads, dynamic location-based delivery targeting, and order tracking — directly to a secure Pharmacist Back-Office where licensed staff review uploaded doctor prescriptions, manage medicine batches, and authorize dispatches.

What I built

Disease-categorized catalog

Direct navigation across Cardiology, Diabetes, Respiratory & Asthma, Neurology, Gastroenterology, and Orthopedics.

1-Click prescription upload

Patients upload digital or photographed doctor prescriptions (Rx) directly for pharmacist verification before dispensing.

Pharmacist Back-Office

A protected portal where pharmacists review patient prescriptions, verify dosages against regulatory standards, and approve dispatches.

Live order & delivery tracking

Patients track order progress with assurances for 4°C cold-chain logistics and genuine batch verification.

Dynamic delivery location pill

Header control allowing patients to switch delivery cities, automatically calculating local delivery timelines.

User auth & Google Sign-In

Session-based customer accounts, registration, and Google OAuth integration for 1-tap patient sign-in.

What this project shows

  • Full-stack web architecture with PHP backend scripts and a relational MySQL schema
  • Regulated e-commerce compliance — separating OTC products from prescription-gated orders
  • Secure file uploading and validation for medical document attachments
  • Live cloud deployment on Alwaysdata with full SSL encryption and responsive mobile optimization
