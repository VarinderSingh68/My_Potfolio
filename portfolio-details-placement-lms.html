GJIMT Placement LMS — college placement portal

A placement-management portal I built for the Placement Cell at my college. The Placement Cell posts a drive once and every eligible student is emailed the details; students apply, mark attendance with a code, and keep their records up to date themselves.

Overview

Placement season at a college has a lot of moving parts: company notices, eligibility lists, emails to the right students, applications, attendance at every drive and talk, and reports at the end. I built the GJIMT Placement LMS for the Placement Cell at Gian Jyoti Institute of Management & Technology — the college where I study for my B.C.A. — so all of that lives in one place, with a separate portal for each role.

It’s a React single-page app built with Vite. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore handle accounts and records, SheetJS powers the Excel and CSV imports and exports, and a small Node.js mail service sends email through Gmail SMTP. Emails are queued before they’re sent, so if the mail server can’t be reached nothing is lost — they wait and can be retried.

Beyond placement drives it also has sections for internships (partners, roles and seats), industry visits (faculty coordinators and student groups) and industry or alumni talks.

What I built

Three role-based portals

Admin, Placement Cell and Student each get their own sign-in and workspace. New Placement Cell accounts wait for the Admin’s approval, and any account can be switched off.

Placement Drive desk

Post a drive straight from the company’s notice — role, JD, package, locations, mode, date, eligible programmes and cut-offs — then edit it and email the update.

Email queue & outbox

Drive emails go out in batches of ten with a sent or failed status for every student. Welcome emails, receipts, application updates and session notices wait in an outbox, and failed ones are retried in one click.

Applications

Eligible students apply from their portal and can withdraw while still “Applied”. The Placement Cell updates statuses in bulk, adds a message and downloads the applicant list.

Code-based attendance

Create a session for a drive, training or talk, open check-in and show a 6-character code. Students mark themselves present; the list refreshes every few seconds.

Student Master Data

Add a student in one form, or import up to 5,000 rows from Excel or CSV with a preview, duplicate handling, and a download of the rows that need fixing.

Linked student sign-up

Students sign up with the email the college has on record and are linked to their record automatically. Email verification codes, Google sign-in and password reset by emailed code.

Reports & activity log

Charts of drives by status, placement interest, specializations and preferred locations, a full Excel report, and an Admin-only log of who signed in and what they did.

What this project shows

  • Role-based access control: three portals, admin approval and per-account switch-off
  • Real-world data handling — bulk Excel/CSV import with validation and duplicate handling, plus Excel exports everywhere
  • A resilient email pipeline: an outbox queue, batching, per-recipient status and retries, so no email is lost if the mail server is down
  • Product thinking for real users — students on phones, staff on desktops — with a light and a dark theme
