About This Project

Doctor Business Growth Agent is a full-stack clinic management platform, demoed here as "Dr. Rana Dental Clinic." It pairs a patient-facing marketing and booking site with an admin dashboard for running the practice.

Patients can browse services with transparent pricing (cleanings, fillings, extractions, root canals, cosmetic procedures, implants, and more), then book an appointment through a calendar and time-slot picker that confirms within 24 hours. The site also includes a live backend status indicator, a patient portal entry point, and a chat widget for quick questions. On the admin side, the dashboard manages appointments, services, and clinic content.
