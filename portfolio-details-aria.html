About This Project

ARIA (Advanced Reasoning & Integration Assistant) is a super-agent control center: a single chat interface where a request gets reasoned about and routed to the right tool automatically, instead of needing a different app for every task.

Built-in shortcuts cover the most common jobs — generating a data visualization, drafting a document, writing and running a script, or drafting and sending a message — and any of them can also be triggered by just describing what you need in plain language. A "Force a specific tool" dropdown lets you override ARIA's own routing when you know exactly what you want, and a Dev Mode caters to IT users who need lower-level control. On the knowledge side, ARIA can ingest files (up to 200MB, TXT/DOCX/PDF and more) or pull in content directly from a URL, so it can answer against your own documents instead of just general knowledge.
