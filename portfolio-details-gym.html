Overview

Gym Business Growth Agent is a membership platform for a fitness club, demoed as “Volt Fitness.” Programs, trainers and pricing all lead to a clear call to action.

Security was a deliberate focus: the admin login is verified on the server, so the password is checked by the backend and never stored in the front-end app.

What I built

Trainer previews

Live trainer cards and membership stats on the homepage.

How we train

An about page with an impact panel, method breakdown and coach spotlight carousel.

Pricing with savings toggle

Basic, Standard and Premium plans, monthly or yearly.

Server-verified admin

Credentials are checked by the backend, never shipped to the browser.

What this project shows

  • React single-page app
  • Conversion-focused page structure and pricing UI
  • Keeping secrets out of the front end with server-side authentication
