About This Project

Gym Business Growth Agent is a full-stack membership platform for a fitness club, demoed here as "Volt Fitness." It's built around getting a visitor from "interested" to "joined" — programs, trainers, and pricing all lead straight to a call to action.

The homepage pairs a strength-training hero with live trainer preview cards and membership stats (classes per week, coach-led hours, recovery tools), and the About page adds an impact panel, a "how we train" method breakdown, and a coach spotlight carousel. Pricing is presented as monthly/yearly plans with a savings toggle across Basic, Standard, and Premium tiers. The admin login is backend-verified — the password is checked server-side and never stored in the front-end app.
