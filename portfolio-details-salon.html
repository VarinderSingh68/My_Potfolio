About This Project

Salon Business Growth Agent is a full-stack booking and e-commerce platform for a luxury salon, demoed here as "Aura." It combines a marketing site, a treatment-booking flow, and a beauty product store with an admin panel behind the scenes.

On the storefront, visitors can browse signature treatments (hair, skin, spa) with pricing and duration, book any service directly from its card, and shop a beauty store with a working cart. On the admin side, the dashboard tracks active services and products, clients, and pending appointments, with dedicated sections for appointments, services, the beauty store, clients, calendar, staff and roles, orders and coupons, marketing, and content/SEO.
