Overview

Salon Business Growth Agent combines a marketing site, a treatment-booking flow and a beauty product store for a luxury salon, demoed as “Aura.”

Visitors browse signature hair, skin and spa treatments with pricing and duration and book directly from any card, or shop the beauty store with a working cart. Behind it, the admin panel is the most complete of the series, with sections for appointments, services, the store, clients, calendar, staff and roles, orders and coupons, marketing, and content/SEO.

What I built

Book from any treatment

Hair, skin and spa services with pricing, duration and a booking button.

Beauty store

Products with a working cart alongside the services.

Staff & roles

Team members and permissions managed in the admin.

Complete admin

Appointments, clients, calendar, orders, coupons, marketing and SEO content.

What this project shows

  • Combining bookings and e-commerce in one product
  • Role-based admin structure
  • Content and SEO tools for non-technical owners
